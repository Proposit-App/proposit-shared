# Release notes — upcoming

## Rejecting a premise from a contradiction now records why

When a review flagged a contradiction and you took the "Reject this premise —
counterexamples exist" way out, the rejection was applied but the reason it
named was thrown away, and the review stopped short of the results instead of
returning you to them. The reason is now recorded with the rejection, and the
review carries on to the results as it should.

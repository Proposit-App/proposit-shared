When publishing is refused because the content breaks Proposit's content policy, read what was refused and either dismiss the refusal or open the appeal page to contest it. The draft is left exactly as it was, so nothing is lost by being refused.

This is the policy sibling of [[see-why-rejected]], not a replacement for it: the two refusals have different causes and different recourse. A grammar rejection lists violations you fix yourself and retry; a policy refusal is a decision you appeal.

Walk through an argument one claim at a time and record a verdict on each — true, false, unknown, or skip — optionally with a reason, building up your review as you advance. Claims are presented in proof order and deduplicated so a shared claim is reviewed once. The walk covers the claims the author wrote: the sources an argument cites and the axioms it rests on are not put to you, so the number of steps is not the argument's total claim count.

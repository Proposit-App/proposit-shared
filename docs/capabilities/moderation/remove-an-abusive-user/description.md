As a moderator, ban a user who violates the zero-tolerance policy for
objectionable content: their contributions come down and the account is locked
out of Proposit so the banned user cannot sign back in. The account itself
remains — a ban is appealable and can be lifted, and lifting it restores exactly
the content the ban hid, leaving anything taken down individually beforehand
still hidden. Banning is not deletion: the banned user's login identity is never
freed for reuse.

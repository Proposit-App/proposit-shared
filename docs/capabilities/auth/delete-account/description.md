Permanently delete your account, behind a confirmation that says so. Your name,
username, email and picture are erased and the login identity is freed, so you
can never sign in with that account again — and the address can be used to
register a fresh one. Arguments you wrote stay published, under a marker byline
rather than yours. Nothing here can be undone; if you only want a break,
[deactivate](tcw://C/auth/deactivate-account) instead.

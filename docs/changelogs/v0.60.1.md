## Fixed

- **`ReviewEngine.setOperatorReason` could not resolve a premise decision that
  carried an `expressionId`.** The identity rule for an operator decision was
  implemented twice and the two copies disagreed: the shared rule keys a
  `scope: "premise"` decision by the bare `premiseId` (one decision per premise;
  the operator's `expressionId` is provenance for the objection, not a scope),
  while `setOperatorReason` re-derived its own rule from `key.split(":")` and
  demanded the row's `expressionId` match the key's — empty for a bare
  `premiseId`. Any caller keying by the premise, as the shared rule says it
  should, hit `Error: setOperatorReason: no op for <premiseId>` whenever the
  stored row carried an operator id. Reported from the web app's
  contradiction-alert "reject this premise" exit: the rejection landed, the
  reason code was dropped, and the throw aborted the jump back to the results
  step.

- The rule now lives in one place, `src/engine/review/operator-key.ts`
  (`operatorAssignmentKey`), and `setOperatorAssignment`, `setOperatorReason`,
  `operatorDecidedKeys` and `LocalStorageReviewStore.upsertOperatorAssignment`
  all resolve through it. `setOperatorReason` still throws on a genuine miss —
  a reason has nothing to attach to without a decision, every caller records the
  decision first, and `setClaimReason` fails the same way.

- Regression test: `src/engine/review/__tests__/review-engine.test.ts` sets a
  premise-scope decision carrying an `expressionId` and asserts the reason lands
  when keyed by the premise.

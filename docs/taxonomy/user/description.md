A Proposit account and one of the central objects of the whole application.

Proposit Users ("Users") are composed of two different underlying database-level objects: user ("db user") and account ("db account").

Users have many fields, including:
- Identity information
  - Display name (ie. legal name)
  - email
  - username
  - image
- Access tier
- LLM token quota
- Persistent preferences

Users are also the owners/creators of arguments and the actors behind reviews, reactions, and tasks.

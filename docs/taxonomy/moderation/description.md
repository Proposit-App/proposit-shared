Enforcing Proposit's content policy against user-authored content.

Two loops, one concern. The **reactive** loop acts after publication: a reader reports content, a moderator triages it, and an argument is hidden, unhidden, or hard-deleted. The **proactive** loop acts at the write boundary: content is screened before it becomes publicly readable, and a violating write is refused rather than published and later removed.

The two compose — screening reduces what reaches the report queue — and neither replaces the other.

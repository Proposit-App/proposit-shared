# Peter Singer

Peter Singer (b. 1946) is an Australian moral philosopher, a leading figure in contemporary applied ethics and a foremost exponent of preference utilitarianism. His 1972 essay "Famine, Affluence, and Morality" and his book _Animal Liberation_ (1975) reshaped debate on global poverty and the moral status of animals, and he is a founding figure of the effective altruism movement. He has taught at Monash University and Princeton University.

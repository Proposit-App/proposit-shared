# John Stuart Mill

John Stuart Mill (1806–1873) was an English philosopher, political economist, and member of Parliament — among the most influential liberal thinkers of the nineteenth century. Rigorously schooled by his father in Jeremy Bentham's utilitarianism, he became both its leading exponent and a searching critic. He is best known for his defense of individual freedom and as an early advocate of women's rights. His major works include On Liberty, Utilitarianism, and The Subjection of Women.
